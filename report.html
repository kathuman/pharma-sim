What the model leaves out

  • One product family, one plant, at most one distributor and one ingredient supplier. Several markets, other products competing for the same line, and allocation between markets are not modelled.
  • Campaigns are averaged: their lost capacity, delay and cycle stock are modelled, but not individual batches and when each is made.
  • The distributor's stock is not aged; shelf life is tracked at the plant only.
  • Nobody is laid off: the workforce shrinks only as people leave.
  • Costs and prices are illustrative, not a quotation.
